# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive project community. Participants should:

- be respectful and professional;
- assume good intent and focus criticism on the work;
- provide actionable technical feedback;
- respect privacy and avoid sharing sensitive information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, malicious disruption, and publication of private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this code and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and constructive.

Report serious conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers rather than opening a public issue.
